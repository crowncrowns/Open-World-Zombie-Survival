# Peeling Brown Paint 01
Красно-коричневая выцветшая краска со сколами до светлой штукатурки по референсу пользователя.
Назначайте M_PeelingBrownPaint_World.mat на стены. SG_PeelingBrownPaint_World.shadergraph — отдельная копия SG_Brick_World с прежней мировой трипланарной проекцией.
Brick_Tile=0.5: один повтор примерно 2×2 м; увеличение значения делает рисунок мельче. Brick_Blend=1, Brick_Smoothness_Mult=1. Масштаб независим от UV и Transform, рисунок привязан к миру.
8 карт 2048×2048: BaseColor (sRGB), Normal_OpenGL (+Y, NormalMap), Height16, AO, Roughness, Metallic (0), MetallicSmoothness (RGB=0, A=1−roughness), ORM (R=AO/G=roughness/B=metallic). Карты данных linear, Repeat U/V, mipmaps, Trilinear, anisotropy8.
В мировом материале подключены BaseColor, Normal, AO, Roughness, Metallic. Исходный SG_Brick_World не имеет Height/Parallax: Height16 сохранена для других шейдеров, не используется этим материалом. Рельеф около0.8мм — художественное допущение; геометрия и коллайдеры не меняются.
Built-in image_gen: цвет и согласованная художественная высота; нормали вычислены из высоты, AO/roughness оценены. Исходники1254×1254 пересчитаны2048×2048; не измеренный скан. Коррекция стыков по обеим осям. Параметры проверок в Validation.json.
Исходный кирпичный граф, сцены, префабы и назначения материалов не изменялись. Play не запускался. Внешний вид на модели проверить вручную.

## Промпты (built-in image_gen)
### BaseColor
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate a square seamless PBR base color texture, full bleed edge to edge, orthographic surface only, of an old Soviet apartment stairwell wall coated in reddish brown burnt sienna oil paint, based on the attached reference: dark rusty red/brown paint with chalk-white plaster visible through small irregular peeling flakes. Aged after three years abandonment: uneven faded terracotta patches, rubbed and abraded paint, scattered chips with brittle peeling edges, subtle dirty gray smudges and fine plaster pores. Most surface retains reddish brown paint; exposed off-white plaster about 12 percent, mostly small flakes with a few medium patches. Realistic scale 2x2 metres. No brick, concrete blocks, metal, rust flakes, wood grain, tile grid, large holes, signage, text, objects, trim or strong horizontal lines. Neutral uniform DELIT albedo only: no directional lighting, no shadows, no specular highlights, no vignette, no perspective. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges, evenly distributed irregular detail. Output one square image only, no margins, no labels.
### Height
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the just generated reddish brown peeling painted wall texture. Produce ONLY a pixel-registered grayscale HEIGHT map of exactly the same square full framing. Keep every flake, crack, exposed white plaster patch and texture location aligned. Painted areas are a mostly flat plateau at 70% gray, exposed plaster slightly lower 40% gray, thin curled paint edges subtly higher 78% gray; fine hairline cracks and pores recessed. Very shallow paint film scale, overall range around 0.8 millimetres. Ignore changes in paint COLOR, dirt and fading when determining height; do not turn stains into hills. No illumination, directional shading, highlights, cast shadows, text, margins or labels. Tileable on four edges. Technical height data only.
